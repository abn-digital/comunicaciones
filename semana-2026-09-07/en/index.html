01 Updates

Signals to act on.

This week, digital advertising platforms continue their push towards AI-driven automation, directly impacting campaign management and agency strategy. Furthermore, there's a redefinition in how agencies structure their services and billing models.

01
Google Ads

Google Migrates Campaigns to AI Max, Discontinues DSA

As of September 1st, Google has begun automatically migrating campaigns with Broad Match and Dynamic Search Ads (DSA) to AI Max. This change also means new DSA campaigns can no longer be created. It is crucial to review account settings before this automatic change is applied to ensure a smooth transition.

AI in Marketing

ChatGPT Ads Reaches $1B Annualized Revenue, Expands

ChatGPT Ads has achieved $1 billion in annualized revenue in less than 200 days since its launch. Now available in 52 countries via self-service, this channel is solidifying its role as an important complement to Google and Meta, though not yet a proven primary channel.

03
Marketing Business

Agency Business Model Evolves Towards Value and Performance

A Forbes/Forrester analysis predicts that the traditional hourly retainer model for agencies is being replaced by value and performance-based pricing. Additionally, "principal media" (reselling inventory with a margin) will gain importance in agency revenue. This is key for reevaluating how agency services are structured and charged.

04
Growth Marketing

Strategy to Exclude Refunded Users from Retargeting Campaigns

A recent Demand Curve newsletter emphasizes the importance of excluding users who have received a refund from retargeting campaigns. This tactic is crucial for optimizing ad spend and ensuring marketing efforts are directed at audiences with a higher likelihood of conversion, thereby improving campaign efficiency.
