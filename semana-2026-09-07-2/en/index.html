01 Updates

Signals to act on.

This week, digital advertising platforms continue to evolve, with Google pushing automation and OpenAI solidifying its presence in the ad market. Additionally, the agency sector is adapting to new pricing models.

Google Ads

Automatic campaign migration to AI Max

Starting September 1st, Google began automatically migrating campaigns with Broad Match and Dynamic Search Ads (DSA) to AI Max. It is no longer possible to create new DSA campaigns. It's crucial to review accounts before these automatic changes are applied and monitor performance.

ChatGPT Ads

OpenAI simplifies ad buying on ChatGPT

OpenAI is working to simplify the ad buying process within ChatGPT. This initiative aims to make it easier for advertisers to manage and optimize their campaigns directly on the platform, opening new opportunities for conversational advertising.

Business Model

Evolution of marketing agency pricing models

The traditional hourly retainer model is losing ground to value- and performance-based pricing schemes. Agencies are adapting to align their incentives with client results, reflecting a shift in market expectations.

ChatGPT Ads

ChatGPT Ads reaches $1B annualized revenue and expands

In less than 200 days since its launch, ChatGPT Ads has reached an annualized revenue of one billion dollars. The platform is now available via self-service in 52 countries, demonstrating its rapid growth and global adoption in the advertising ecosystem.
