02 What Re·Adapt is

Generate and adapt creatives with AI.

Re·Adapt is ABN Digital’s platform to produce ad creatives in a flow: brief → generation → campaign format adaptation → precise polish. Built for brand and performance teams that need volume without losing creative control.

01

Canvas

Chain steps (Start, Generate, Adapt, Batch, Sketch) and run the full flow with credit cost visible upfront.

02

Adapt to formats

From one piece to 1:1, 4:5, 9:16, 16:9 and the rest of the campaign mix — several images at once.

03

Refine

Point to an element and describe the change: region-based polish, with version history.

04

Team and client

Credits, creatives, and canvases live per brand/client. Share canvases or images with your team.

Node details, per-generation costs, and best practices are in the Re·Adapt manual.

03 How to start

App + access via your Digital team.

Open the platform and, if you don’t have access yet, ask your Account Manager or the Re·Adapt admin at ABN Digital. They enable the account and the matching plan.
